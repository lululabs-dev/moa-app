Frequently asked questions

Know what to expect.

What links and images can I save?

You can save standard web links and screenshots selected from Photos. Pages that block access or do not provide readable content may fail to organize. In that case, save screenshots of the content instead.

Where are my scraps stored?

Originals are saved on this device first. If you enable iCloud sync, they also sync to your private iCloud database. Content is sent to a processing service during AI organization, but the source is not retained on our servers afterward.

What happens if I delete the app or change devices?

Local scraps that have not synced to iCloud are removed with the app. Before reinstalling or switching devices, enable iCloud sync or export a manual backup from Settings.

How do I manage Premium?

Apple handles payment, trials, renewal, and cancellation. You can restore a purchase in MOA Settings and change or cancel your plan in your Apple Account subscription settings.
