FAQ

Before you start

What can I save?

Web links and screenshots from your Photos library. When a page can’t be read — a login wall, for example — MOA tells you so instead of guessing.

Where are my scraps stored?

On your device first. With iCloud sync on, they are also kept in your own private iCloud, which we neither store nor read. With sync off, they stay on this device only.

What if I delete the app or switch phones?

With iCloud sync on, everything restores under the same account. If you’d rather not use sync, you can export a backup file instead.

Is there an Android version?

An Android version is on the way. When it ships, cloud sync will use MOA’s private app storage in your own Google Drive — and, as today, we will neither store nor read it.

How do I manage my subscription?

Cancel anytime in App Store subscription settings, and use Restore Purchases in the app to reapply it.
